SEEKER OF SOUNDZ — COMPLETE WEBSITE FEATURE SUMMARY
Version: 4.10.17

1. CORE BRAND & VISUAL EXPERIENCE
- Seeker Of SoundZ artist/DJ identity, shared SOS logo, cinematic loaders and page transitions.
- Frequency Interface visual language with animated navigation, responsive cards, cursor effects, and star particles.
- Account-specific Experience Studio with backgrounds, starfield levels, transitions, loader styles, motion, contrast, cursor glow, and card glow.
- Guest defaults restore on sign-out; each member's visual profile restores at sign-in.
- Member visibility preferences can hide Frequency Updates and announcement banners.

2. NAVIGATION & SEARCH
- Shared responsive navigation across pages.
- Animated active-page indicator, hover frequency line, glowing account/cart controls.
- Universal expandable search for pages, forums, merch, music, videos/YouTube, plugins, members, events, and administration.
- Keyboard support including Escape, arrows, Enter, and Ctrl/Cmd+K.

3. MEMBERS & PROFILES
- Registration/login demonstration, profile dashboard, badges, achievements, activity, VIP and collaboration access.
- Personal Experience Studio with live previews and Save & Apply behavior.
- Per-account settings storage and guest-default restoration.
- Optional hiding of site update controls and announcement banners.

4. FORUMS
- Topic creation, categories, subcategories, tags, custom tags, images, emoji tools, search and sorting.
- Replies, likes, multi-emoji reply reactions, add/remove reaction behavior, moderation, pin/lock/hide/solve.
- Member profiles, roles, badges, achievements, reputation, view counts and delete confirmations.
- Signed-out users remain on the forum and receive a sign-in notice instead of being redirected.

5. MUSIC & LIVE FREQUENCY DJ STAGE
- Music releases, previews, access tiers, purchase links, full-release files and metadata.
- Members-only DJ concert stage with selectable waveforms and visualizers.
- Playlist, previous/next, loop-current, stop-after-current, and full-queue modes.
- Blackout focus mode, lasers, moving spotlights, LED wall, fog, bass rings, sparks, confetti, crowd glow and accessibility-conscious strobe controls.
- Multiple concert presets, reset controls, test track, performance-mode integration.
- Laser color designer supporting random multi-color or solid violet, cyan, magenta, green, gold, red, blue, or white beams.

6. MERCH STORE
- Apparel and digital/music product categories, quick view, image zoom, sizes, colors, quantity and cart.
- Category-aware admin editor: music/digital products hide clothing sizes/colors; apparel retains them.
- Featured, limited, seasonal, new release, preorder and members-only badges.

7. COLLABORATION STUDIO
- Private collaboration access, projects, stages, progress, team management, messages, audio/version uploads and storage recovery tools.
- Admin collaboration moderation and deep links to specific project messages.
- Performance safeguards for large browser-stored audio/chat histories.

8. ADMIN HUB
- Dashboard analytics and management for users, forums, announcements, merch, music, gallery, videos, events and collaboration teams.
- Announcement targeting, styles, priorities, placements, dismissibility and expiration.
- Performance/storage diagnostics and cleanup/export tools.
- Site Experience defaults for guests/new accounts.

9. MEDIA, EVENTS & SOCIAL
- Gallery categories and lightbox-style presentation.
- Videos, featured media and admin-managed embeds.
- Events, upcoming/past appearances and event management.
- Social hub and contact/booking pages.
- Producer Hub with a large searchable FL Studio/DJ resource directory.

10. PERFORMANCE & ACCESSIBILITY
- Performance Mode disables star particles and heavier audio-reactive effects.
- Reduced-motion handling, page visibility pausing, capped animation workloads and lazy media behavior.
- Responsive mobile layouts, touch targets, keyboard focus, modal layering and custom-cursor fallbacks.

11. STORAGE & PRODUCTION FOUNDATION
- Browser-based demo storage for accounts, profiles, posts, reactions, catalog, music, announcements and collaboration data.
- Export/import and storage-management tools.
- Documentation and example backend/payment webhook foundation for production migration.

IMPORTANT
This package remains primarily a static/browser-storage demonstration. Real secure authentication, shared multi-user data, payments, durable uploads and production administration require a server-side backend and database.
