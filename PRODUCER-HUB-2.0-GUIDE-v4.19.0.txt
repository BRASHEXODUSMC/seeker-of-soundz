PRODUCER HUB 2.0 QUICK GUIDE

1. Open Video Effects Studio and choose Create.
2. Upload a video and a licensed music file.
3. Open Preset Packs or Assets in the left sidebar and apply effects.
4. Select Analyze Beats to generate waveform and beat markers.
5. Use Auto-Sync Effects for a quick beat-reactive setup.
6. Adjust timing, volume, layers, and export settings in Inspector.
7. Use Render Project or Add to Render Queue.
8. Completed Video Download API jobs appear under Downloads and attempt Import.

Browser guidance:
- Chrome/Edge: strongest rendering and tab-audio capture support.
- Firefox: local upload, waveform, effects, timeline, WEBM rendering, and project tools are supported; tab audio capture depends on OS/source.
- MP4 export only appears when MediaRecorder reports support; WEBM remains the reliable fallback.
