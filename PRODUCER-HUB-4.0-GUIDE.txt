PRODUCER HUB 4.0 QUICK GUIDE

1. IMPORT
- Open Videos.
- Choose or import a local video.
- Music is optional for visual-effect preview.

2. EFFECTS
- Open Assets.
- Search the Complete Effects Library.
- Select ADD to activate an effect.
- Effects update on paused frames and animate while playing.

3. LOGO AND TEXT
- Upload a logo under Assets.
- Select position, size and opacity.
- Enter overlay text and choose an EDM or retro style.

4. YOUTUBE
- Open YouTube Tools.
- Search YouTube using the new search field.
- Copy a selected video URL.
- Paste it into the existing embedded preview.
- Use the external converter only for media you own or are authorized to download.

5. TIMELINE
- Click the ruler or a lane to seek.
- Drag the playhead.
- Click track headers to open related tools.
- Use Arrow keys, Home and End while the playhead is focused.

6. THEATRE VIEW
- Select Theatre View.
- The surrounding website darkens and blurs.
- Press Escape to exit.

7. WAVEFORMS
- Import a local video or audio file.
- Open Waveforms.
- Download PNG, SVG, CSV, JSON, beat markers or WAV where supported.

8. EXPORT
- Confirm video, effects, logo and text.
- Add licensed music when desired.
- Render the project.
