PRODUCER HUB 4.1 QUICK GUIDE

TEXT AND LOGOS
1. Open Assets.
2. Enter text or upload a logo.
3. Choose a font, preset position, or Custom X/Y.
4. Set colors, outline, glow, background, rotation and spacing.
5. Preview and render normally.

WAVEFORM OVER VIDEO
1. Upload a local audio or video file with audio.
2. Enable Show waveform over video.
3. Select Line, Bars, Mirrored or Circle.
4. Choose color, position, height and opacity.

CAMERA MOTION
1. Open Camera Motion & Transitions under Assets.
2. Select a camera-motion preset.
3. Adjust Motion Strength.
4. The actual video frame moves; logos, text and effects remain layered above it.

TRANSITIONS
1. Select separate Intro and Outro transitions.
2. Adjust each duration.
3. Preview from the beginning or near the end.
4. Transitions are included in the direct finished-video render.

CUT TOOL
1. Move the playhead to the beginning of the unwanted section.
2. Select Mark Cut Start.
3. Move the playhead to the end.
4. Select Mark Cut End.
5. Select Remove Section.
6. Use Undo, Redo, Clear Cuts, or click a cut region to restore it.
7. Cuts are non-destructive and are skipped during preview and export.
