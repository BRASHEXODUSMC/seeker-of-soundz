PRODUCER HUB 4.2 MULTI-LAYER GUIDE

1. Open Assets > Multi-Layer Canvas Editor.
2. Select + Text or + Image. The image picker accepts several files at once.
3. Drag a selected layer directly over the video.
4. Drag the circular ↻ handle around the layer to rotate it.
5. Use the property editor for exact X, Y, rotation, scale and opacity.
6. Choose independent entrance and exit transitions.
7. Choose a per-layer visual effect.
8. Move layers up or down to control their final stacking order.
9. Duplicate, hide, show or delete layers as needed.
10. Render normally. All visible extra layers are included in the download.
