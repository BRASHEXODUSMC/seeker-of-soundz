PRODUCER HUB 4.2.1 QUICK GUIDE

UPLOAD YOUR OWN MUSIC
1. Open the Music tab.
2. Select Upload Your Own Music.
3. Choose an MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A or browser-readable audio file.
4. You can also drag the audio directly onto the Music shelf.
5. The track becomes available for waveform analysis, preview and export.

REAL-TIME LAYER MOVEMENT
1. Select a text, logo or image layer.
2. Hold the mouse button and drag continuously.
3. Do not release until the layer is in position.
4. The canvas and X/Y values update while moving.

REAL-TIME ROTATION
1. Select or hover over a layer.
2. Hold the circular ↻ handle.
3. Drag around the layer continuously.
4. The layer rotates without requiring another click.
5. The exact rotation value updates during movement.
