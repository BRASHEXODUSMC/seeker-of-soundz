PRODUCER HUB 4.3 QUICK GUIDE

LAYER EDITOR
1. Open Assets.
2. The logo/text and multi-layer controls now remain inside the sidebar.
3. Scroll inside the selected-layer editor to reach every property.
4. Drag and rotate layers over the preview as before.

MULTIPLE VIDEOS
1. Open Videos.
2. Import Video 1 with the normal chooser.
3. Select Add Multiple Video Clips to add Video 2, Video 3, or more.
4. Drag clip cards to reorder them.
5. Click a clip card or timeline segment to preview it.
6. The project previews and renders every clip in order.

MUTING
- Select the speaker beside an individual clip to mute only that clip.
- Use Mute All Clips or the Video timeline speaker for every clip.
- Use the Music timeline speaker for uploaded music.
- Use the Effects timeline button for timed effects.

TIMED EFFECTS
1. Move the playhead to the desired start.
2. Choose an effect.
3. Select Mark Effect Start.
4. Move the playhead to the ending position.
5. Select Mark Effect End.
6. Select Add Effect Region.
7. Keep Snap to nearest beat marker enabled for beat-aligned timing.
8. Click a region to mute it, or select × to delete it.

CUTS
Existing cut, undo and redo tools now use the combined multiclip timeline.
