PRODUCER HUB 5.0 QUICK GUIDE

AUTO-SYNC CAMERA AND EFFECTS
1. Upload music and video.
2. Select Analyze Beats.
3. Select an Auto-Sync pattern, density and camera strength.
4. Select Auto-Sync Effects.
5. If beat analysis was not finished, Auto-Sync continues automatically afterward.
6. Green camera regions show actual video-frame movement.
7. Purple regions show timed visual effects.

PLACE AN EFFECT ON ONE BEAT
1. Click a beat marker in the Beat Markers lane.
2. Choose an effect.
3. Choose Beat Length.
4. Select Place on Selected Beat.

TIMELINE ZOOM
- Use + and − or the zoom slider.
- Zoom stays centered around the playhead.
- Select Fit to show the complete project.
- Scroll horizontally with the wheel while zoomed.

RESIZE LANES
- Drag the thin divider under Video, Music, Effects, or Beat Markers.
- Video height also expands automatically for V1, V2, V3, and later lanes.

CLIP TRANSITIONS
1. Open Videos.
2. Drag a transition preset.
3. Drop it onto the connector between two clip blocks.
4. Click a configured connector to remove its transition.
