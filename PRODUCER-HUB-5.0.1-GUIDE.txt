PRODUCER HUB 5.0.1 QUICK GUIDE

TIMELINE NAVIGATION
- Mouse wheel: scroll vertically through timeline lanes.
- Shift + mouse wheel: scroll horizontally.
- Horizontal trackpad gesture: scroll horizontally.
- Ctrl/Cmd + wheel: zoom around the playhead.
- Hold middle mouse button and drag: pan horizontally and vertically.
- The ruler remains visible while scrolling down.

FIND BEAT MARKERS
1. Open the Timeline.
2. Scroll downward with the normal mouse wheel.
3. Beat Markers are located beneath Timed Effects.
4. Click a marker to select it and place effects directly on that beat.

FIRE, EXPLOSION AND SMOKE
1. Open Assets or the Effects module.
2. Find Fire, Explosion & Smoke VFX.
3. Enable one or more procedural effects.
4. Preview normally.
5. These effects are included in the direct final render.
6. They can also be added as timed effect regions or placed on one beat.
