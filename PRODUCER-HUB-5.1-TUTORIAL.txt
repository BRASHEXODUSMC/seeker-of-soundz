PRODUCER HUB 5.1 TUTORIAL

RIPPLE CUT A RANGE
1. Load Video 1 and any additional videos.
2. Move the playhead to the unwanted section's beginning.
3. Select Mark Cut Start.
4. Move the playhead to its ending.
5. Select Mark Cut End.
6. Keep Ripple Close Gaps enabled.
7. Select Remove Section.
8. The remaining footage and later videos move left to fill the gap.

CUT ACROSS VIDEOS
- Mark the start near the end of one video.
- Mark the end inside the following video.
- Remove the section.
- The surviving first-video footage connects directly to the surviving
  portion of the following video.

SNAPPING
- Snap Cuts to Video Edges aligns cut marks near clip boundaries.
- Tight, Normal and Wide control the snapping range.
- Disable cut snapping for unrestricted placement.
- Snap Playhead to Clip Edges controls transport and timeline movement.

UNDO AND REDO
- Undo restores the earlier multiclip sequence.
- Redo applies the ripple edit again.
- Uploaded source files are not permanently modified.

TRANSPORT
- Restart returns to project time zero.
- Rewind moves backward five seconds.
- Play previews the edited multiclip project.
- Pause stops video and music.
- Forward moves ahead five seconds.
