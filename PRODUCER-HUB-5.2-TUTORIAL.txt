PRODUCER HUB 5.2 QUICK GUIDE

RESTORE BEAT MARKERS
1. Upload your music.
2. Select Analyze Beats.
3. Wait for the marker count confirmation.
4. Scroll to the Beat Markers lane.
5. Purple markers are normal beats; green markers are stronger/drop beats.

AUTO-SYNC
1. Analyze the music, then select Auto-Sync Effects.
2. Auto-Sync can also be selected first; analysis continues automatically.
3. Timed visual regions appear in the Effects lane.
4. Camera regions move the actual video frame.

SHIFT+K VIDEO RAZOR
1. Hover the pointer over the exact video timeline position.
2. Press Shift+K.
3. The video becomes two source-aware sections.
4. Hover the split boundary.
5. Select ×L or R× to delete one side.

SHIFT+K MUSIC RAZOR
1. Hover over the music waveform.
2. Press Shift+K.
3. Hover the music split boundary.
4. Select ×L or R×.
5. Double-click a removed music section to restore it.
