PRODUCER HUB 5.3 QUICK GUIDE

OVERLAPPING AUTO-SYNC
1. Upload music and analyze beats.
2. Choose an Auto-Sync camera pattern and density.
3. Choose one to four Overlays Per Beat.
4. Select the effect pools Auto-Sync may use.
5. Select Auto-Sync Effects.
6. Camera regions move the actual video while multiple overlay regions may overlap.

REACTIVE PARTICLES
Enable Reactive Particle Field, Bass Sparks, Beat Dust, Frequency Orbs,
Drop Confetti, or Audio Light Trails. Their movement and brightness respond
to bass, mids, highs and peak energy.

RENDER FOCUS
1. Keep Show Only the Render Workspace enabled.
2. Begin rendering.
3. The timeline and editor panels hide until rendering ends.
4. Keep browser hardware acceleration enabled.
5. The browser and graphics driver decide which canvas operations use the GPU.
6. Close unnecessary tabs and avoid resizing the window during rendering.

ORIGINAL TEXT
1. Enter text in Overlay Text.
2. Use all existing font, color, outline, glow and transition controls.
3. Drag the selection box directly over the video.
4. Drag the circular rotation handle to rotate.
