PRODUCER HUB 5.5 QUICK GUIDE

PLAYBACK
1. Select Play beneath the video.
2. The playhead follows the complete edited project.
3. It continues across trimmed segments and later videos.

START AND END RANGE
1. Drag the green Start handle beneath the timeline ruler.
2. Drag the red End handle.
3. Preview and finished rendering use only the highlighted range.
4. Snapping can align the handles to clip edges or detected beats.

TRIMMED VIDEO
Ripple-cut and Shift+K sections preserve their original source positions.
Preview and rendering seek to the surviving position before playback begins,
so deleted footage is not shown.

BASS-DROP DISTORTION
1. Analyze Beats.
2. Choose Bass Drop Distortion, Bass Warp & Crush, or Drop Impact Cinema.
3. Keep Distort Picture on Strong Bass Drops enabled.
4. Choose Mixed, Wave Warp, Digital Crush, Lens Punch, or Twist & Rotate.
5. Select Auto-Sync Effects.
