PRODUCER HUB 6.0 QUICK GUIDE

1. LOAD MEDIA
Upload Video 1, additional videos/camera angles, and licensed music.

2. TRANSPORT AND TIMELINE
The timeline sits directly below Restart, -5, Play, Pause and +5.
The playhead moves only across the actual clip-bar area.

3. ADVANCED TIMELINE TOOLS
Open the collapsed Advanced Timeline Tools drawer for Timed Effects,
Auto-Sync, Cut & Edit, Normal/Ripple/Magnetic modes, Razor and Trim.

4. AI CAMERA DIRECTOR
Analyze Beats, open Inspector > Pro Suite > AI Director, choose a style and
intensity, and generate a full camera pass.

5. GPU TIMELINE
Keep GPU Cache enabled for timeline containment and OffscreenCanvas support
when available. Hardware acceleration must be enabled in the browser.

6. KEYFRAMES AND CURVES
Add keyframes at the playhead, then choose Linear, Ease, Bounce, Elastic or
Cubic interpolation.

7. AUDIO DUCKING
Enable ducking and adjust amount, threshold, attack and release.

8. MULTICAMERA
Select camera angles during playback. Enable Record Angle Changes to save
switches at the current timeline time.

9. PROJECT SAVE
Download a settings-only project or enable Embed Local Video/Audio for a
portable project. Portable files can be extremely large.

10. IMPORT
Import .sosproject to continue a project or .sospreset to restore only
settings and animation choices.

11. RENDER
Use Render Focus and keep hardware acceleration enabled. The browser decides
which canvas work uses the GPU versus CPU.
