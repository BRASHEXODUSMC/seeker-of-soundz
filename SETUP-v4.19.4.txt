SEEKER OF SOUNDZ v4.19.4 SETUP

1. Upload the v4.19.4 files to GitHub Pages.
2. Wait for GitHub Pages to deploy.
3. Open the website and press Ctrl + F5.
4. Sign in and open Members > Profile Settings.
5. Change a setting and select Save & apply to my account.
6. Confirm a success toast appears.
7. In Loader Builder, disable Loader completion swipe and save.
8. Reload the site. No legacy scan or swipe should cross the loader.
9. Enable it and save only when you want the custom final completion swipe.
10. Hover over navigation links to confirm the bottom highlight appears only while hovered, focused, active, or on the current page.

No Supabase update is needed.
