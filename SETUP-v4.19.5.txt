SEEKER OF SOUNDZ v4.19.5 SETUP

1. Upload all v4.19.5 files to GitHub Pages.
2. Wait for deployment and press Ctrl + F5.
3. The first v4.19.5 visit resets loader-only preferences to the clean baseline.
4. Sign in and open Members.
5. Select the Settings module.
6. Open Loader Builder and choose only the loader elements and effects you want.
7. Select Save & apply to my account.
8. Confirm the save toast appears above all modules.
9. Reload the site to test the saved loader.
10. The completion swipe appears only when Loader completion swipe is enabled.

No Supabase changes are required.
