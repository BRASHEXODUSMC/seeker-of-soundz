SEEKER OF SOUNDZ v4.19.6 SETUP

1. Upload all v4.19.6 files to GitHub Pages.
2. Wait for deployment and press Ctrl + F5.
3. Sign in and verify the Members login form does not flash before the dashboard.
4. Open Members > Settings > Loader Builder.
5. Choose a Logo surround effect such as Original Spinning Cubes or Circular Waveform.
6. Save and reload to test the selected effect.
7. Open Collaboration Studio.
8. Select a project you belong to.
9. Confirm the reply field appears directly beneath the newest message.
10. Send a reply and confirm the other collaborator receives the existing project notification.

No Supabase update is required.
