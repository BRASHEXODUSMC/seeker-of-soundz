Seeker Of SoundZ v4.17.12
LICENSED EDM VIDEO LIBRARY + CAPTURE MODULE CONTAINMENT

Licensed EDM Visual Library:
- Adds a dedicated source library inside Video Effects Studio.
- Includes EDM-friendly searches for abstract neon footage, DJ/festival footage, lasers, smoke, particles, music visualizers, glitch overlays, cyberpunk loops, transitions and nightlife.
- Sources include Pexels, Pixabay, Mixkit and YouTube Creative Commons.
- Each card displays the applicable source license or verification requirement.
- Pexels cards link to the Pexels License.
- Pixabay cards link to the Pixabay Content License summary.
- Mixkit cards warn that each item must be checked for Free License versus Restricted License.
- YouTube cards are limited to Creative Commons Attribution videos and require attribution.
- Adds a copyable YouTube CC attribution template.
- Adds source filter buttons.
- The library does not claim that Standard YouTube License videos are reusable.

Capture module containment:
- Hard-limits the tab-capture module, canvas, waveform and preview to the module width.
- Prevents horizontal canvas overflow.
- Adds min-width:0 to all grid and flex children involved in the editor.
- Uses layout/paint containment for the capture preview.
- Compact mode is capped at 760px.
- Expanded mode remains at 100% of the module, never wider than the page.
- Effects controls remain visible beside the preview on desktop.
- The workspace collapses to one column below 980px.
- Canvases scale visually without changing their internal recording resolution.

No Supabase update is required.
