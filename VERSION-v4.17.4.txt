Seeker Of SoundZ v4.17.4
YOUTUBE SYNCHRONIZED EFFECTS WORKFLOW

YouTube mode:
- Loads YouTube through the official iframe Player API.
- Synchronizes a generated visual waveform to the YouTube player's current time and duration.
- Draws selected visual effects live over the embedded YouTube player.
- Supports presets and all existing procedural effects in the live overlay.
- Lets members download the synchronized waveform guide as PNG.
- Keeps YouTube URL copy and Open on YouTube controls.
- Adds Use Original File for Final Export.
- Selecting the original file preserves the selected effects and timeline values, then enables exact waveform analysis and final MP4/WEBM rendering.

Accuracy and export:
- The YouTube-synchronized waveform is a deterministic visual guide, not an audio-derived waveform.
- YouTube's protected iframe does not expose audio samples or video pixels to the website.
- A final edited copy of the YouTube iframe cannot be rendered or downloaded by browser canvas.
- Exact waveform generation and a downloadable edited video require the original local file or a direct cross-origin-enabled MP4/WEBM source.
- This avoids downloading or bypassing protected YouTube media.

Supabase:
- No SQL, Storage bucket, MIME rule, or file-path update is required.
