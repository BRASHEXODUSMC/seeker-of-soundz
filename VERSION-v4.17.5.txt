Seeker Of SoundZ v4.17.5
YOUTUBE PROJECT PICKER + PLAYED WAVEFORM EXPORTS + EXPANDED FX PRESETS

YouTube Project Picker:
- Adds a YouTube project-selection panel above the studio.
- Search terms open YouTube results in a new tab.
- Paste URL from Clipboard loads the selected video immediately.
- Recently used YouTube projects are saved locally with thumbnails.
- Use This Video reloads any recent YouTube project into the embedded preview.
- YouTube remains embedded directly above the waveform and native-video editor.

Waveform exports:
- Played waveform values are recorded approximately every 100 milliseconds.
- Download Played Waveform CSV exports time, bass, mid, high and peak values.
- Download Waveform PNG exports the visible waveform at its current playback position.
- Download Guide WAV creates a synthetic audio guide derived from the synchronized visual envelope.
- The guide WAV does not contain or extract YouTube audio.
- After selecting the original video file, Download Exact Audio WAV exports the decoded audio from that supplied file.
- Exact WAV export is unavailable until a local or cross-origin-readable original source is provided.

Additional effects:
- Adds Digital Rain, Snow Noise, Horizon Glow, Circle Spectrum, Grid Flash and Chromatic Pulse.
- Adds Riddim Reactor, Tearout Chaos, Melodic Glow, Drum & Bass Rush, Color Bass, Retro Club, Minimal Visualizer and Maximum FX presets.
- Existing effects and presets remain unchanged.

Page-position fix:
- Non-submit studio button actions preserve the current scroll coordinates.
- Empty # links are prevented from jumping to the top or bottom of the document.
- All new controls use explicit button types.
- Recent project selection and preset changes remain in the current viewport.

YouTube restriction:
- Embedded YouTube audio and video frames are protected and cannot be exported as an edited video or exact audio WAV from browser JavaScript.
- The original file workflow remains the path for exact waveform analysis, WAV export and final MP4/WEBM rendering.

Supabase:
- No SQL or Storage update is required.
