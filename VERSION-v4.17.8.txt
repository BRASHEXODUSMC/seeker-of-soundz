Seeker Of SoundZ v4.17.8
AUTHORIZED TAB CAPTURE + REAL WAVEFORM RECORDER

New browser-tab capture converter:
- Adds Start Tab Capture for user-authorized browser sharing.
- The member selects a browser tab and enables Share tab audio.
- The shared picture is drawn to a processing canvas.
- Selected visual effects are applied to the captured tab preview and final recording.
- The captured audio stream drives a real live waveform.
- Record with Selected Effects produces a downloadable WEBM containing the processed video and captured audio.
- Captured PCM samples are exported as an actual WAV file.
- The real waveform can be downloaded as PNG.
- Capture status shows whether tab audio was included.
- Every capture requires explicit browser permission.
- The feature requires HTTPS and a browser supporting getDisplayMedia.

Supported selected effects in tab-capture rendering include:
- TV Static

- Vignette

- Waveform / Oscilloscope overlay
- Spectrum Bars

- Hue Shift

YouTube and protected media:
- The website does not directly download, extract, or bypass protected YouTube media.
- A YouTube URL remains an embedded preview.
- Members may explicitly share a browser tab they are authorized to record.
- Members are reminded to record only media they own or have permission to use.
