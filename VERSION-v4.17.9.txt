Seeker Of SoundZ v4.17.9
YOUTUBE AUTOPLAY + STREAMLINED CAPTURE + PROJECT DELETION

YouTube playback:
- Selected YouTube projects now initialize through the YouTube Player API and automatically begin muted playback when the player is ready.
- Adds Play Selected Video so the member can unmute and begin playback with one browser-authorized click.
- This avoids browser autoplay restrictions that prevent sound from starting without a direct user action.
- Existing synchronized waveform and effect overlays continue to follow the embedded player.

YouTube Project Picker:
- Every saved project now has Use This Video and Delete controls.
- Adds Clear Saved Videos to remove the entire recent project list.
- Deleting a saved project does not affect the YouTube video itself or any other site content.
- Saved projects remain local to the member's browser.

Streamlined YouTube capture:
- Adds Capture This YouTube Project inside the embedded-player controls.
- The selected YouTube video opens in a dedicated playback tab.
- The browser share dialog opens immediately afterward.
- The member must still choose the playback tab and enable Share tab audio.
- Once permission is granted, recording starts automatically with the selected effects.
- Captured video, real captured-audio WAV and real waveform PNG remain downloadable after recording stops.

Browser security:
- Browsers do not permit websites to silently capture a tab.
- The share dialog and member approval cannot be removed or bypassed.
- Sound autoplay also requires a user click; the new Play Selected Video button satisfies that requirement.

Supabase:
- No SQL, Storage bucket, MIME policy or file-path update is required.
