Seeker Of SoundZ v4.20.0
PRODUCER HUB 3.1 — EFFECTS, THEATRE, LAYERS & WAVEFORMS

Producer Hub cursor:
- Disables the custom Seeker Of SoundZ cursor only while the pointer is inside Producer Hub.
- Restores the normal site cursor after leaving the Hub.
- Theatre View also uses the native browser cursor.
- Other website pages retain the member-selected custom cursor.

Live visual effects:
- Repairs the local preview canvas so selected effects appear over the imported video.
- Effects can preview without requiring a separate music file.
- Selecting or adjusting an effect refreshes a paused frame immediately.
- Playing the video runs the effects continuously.
- Effects are included in the finished local render.
- Adds procedural support for static, VHS, scanlines, strobe, drop flash,
  waveform, oscilloscope, particles, light leaks, glitches, digital rain,
  cinema bars, horizon glow, grids and other existing selections.

Assets & Layers:
- Adds every existing effect from the previous Effects step to the Assets sidebar.
- Includes search, active state, effect descriptions and Clear All.
- Adds uploaded logo layers.
- Logo options include position, scale, opacity and visibility.
- Adds editable text overlays.
- Text styles include Retro Future, Arcade Pixel, Cyber EDM, Rave Glow,
  Minimal Wide and Terminal.
- Text and logo layers appear in preview and finished exports.

Workspace:
- Adds a new six-step Producer Hub workflow:
  1. Workspace
  2. Assets & Layers
  3. YouTube & Converter
  4. Audio & Waveforms
  5. Theatre & Timeline
  6. Export
- The old duplicate modular area remains hidden for compatibility.
- The older YouTube Project Picker was removed.
- The older synchronized YouTube preview is hidden.
- The new YouTube preview and external converter remain.

YouTube:
- Adds a YouTube search field that opens YouTube search results.
- Members can select a video, copy its URL and paste it into the existing preview.
- The existing Copy URL button remains.
- Cross-origin YouTube players do not allow the website to alter their native right-click menu.

Timeline:
- Timeline rows and headers are keyboard accessible and clickable.
- Clicking Video opens video controls.
- Clicking Music opens audio controls.
- Clicking Effects opens Assets & Layers.
- Clicking Beat Markers opens waveform tools.
- Existing seek, playhead drag, transport, zoom, markers and effects lanes remain.

Theatre View:
- Adds a focused full-window editor mode.
- Darkens and blurs the surrounding website.
- Keeps the sidebar, inspector, editor and timeline inside the focused workspace.
- Escape exits Theatre View.

Waveforms:
- Analyzes audio from imported local video or audio files when the browser supports the codec.
- Adds SVG waveform export.
- Adds CSV peak-data export.
- Adds JSON analysis export.
- Adds extracted mono WAV export.
- Keeps the existing PNG waveform and beat-marker JSON downloads.
- YouTube previews remain reference-only; exact waveform/audio exports require a local file.

Layout:
- Enlarges and spaces the left sidebar.
- Changes clipped two-column sidebar navigation into a single-column layout.
- Prevents Downloads, Waveforms and other labels from being cut off.
- Improves responsive layout for tablets and mobile.

Supabase:
- No new SQL, Edge Function, Storage bucket, secret or RLS update is required.
