Seeker Of SoundZ v4.25.1
PRODUCER HUB 5.0.1 — TIMELINE SCROLLING & PROCEDURAL VFX

Timeline scrolling:
- Timeline viewport now scrolls vertically and horizontally
- Normal mouse wheel scrolls down through all timeline lanes
- Shift + wheel scrolls sideways
- Horizontal trackpad movement remains supported
- Ctrl/Cmd + wheel zooms around the playhead
- Middle mouse button can be held and dragged to pan in any direction
- Smooth inertial movement remains for both axes
- Vertical and horizontal scrollbars remain inside the timeline module
- Timeline ruler stays visible while scrolling down
- Beat Marker lane has increased minimum height and remains reachable
- Clicking a beat marker scrolls its lane into view
- Existing timeline containment, zoom, cuts, lane resizing and clip lanes remain

New built-in procedural VFX:
- Drawn Fire
- Fire Burst
- Ember Storm
- Drawn Explosion
- Impact Explosion
- Explosion Shockwave
- Rolling Smoke
- Smoke Swipe Left
- Smoke Swipe Right
- Smoke Rise
- Purple Rave Smoke
- Fire & Smoke Combo

VFX behavior:
- Effects are generated in the browser with the existing canvas renderer
- No external copyrighted footage or downloaded overlay files are included
- Effects animate during preview and direct project rendering
- Effects can be used globally, inside timed effect regions, on selected beats,
  and with the existing Auto-Sync workflow
- Effects react to bass, mids, highs and peak energy where appropriate

Preserved:
- Pro timeline
- True camera Auto-Sync
- Multiple video lanes and clip transitions
- Direct finished-video rendering
- Timed effects and beat placement
- Multilayer text, logo and image editor
- Waveform tools
- YouTube tools
- Theatre View and custom cursor

No Supabase update is required.
