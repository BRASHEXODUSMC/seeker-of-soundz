Ripple multiclip editing:
- Cut ranges modify the video sequence rather than only hiding playback time
- Remaining source portions become accurate trimmed segments
- Following videos move left immediately and fill the removed gap
- Cuts can begin in one video and end inside another video
- A middle cut can split one source video into two surviving segments
- Source start and end positions are preserved for preview and final rendering
- Combined project duration shrinks after ripple deletion
- Timeline widths update to the edited duration
- Clip transitions and mute states remain attached to surviving segments

Cut controls:
- Ripple Close Gaps can be enabled or disabled
- Snap Cuts to Video Edges can be enabled or disabled
- Tight, Normal and Wide snap ranges
- Snap to Video 1, Video 2, Video 3, and later clip boundaries
- Existing non-destructive cut behavior remains when ripple mode is disabled

Undo and redo:
- Ripple edits use project snapshots
- Existing Undo and Redo controls restore or repeat ripple changes
- Original uploaded source files remain untouched

Transport dock:
- Controls moved below the video preview and closer to the timeline
- Restart
- Rewind five seconds
- Play
- Pause
- Forward five seconds
- Combined project time and duration
- Optional playhead snapping to clip edges

Preserved:
