Beat analysis restored:
- Adds one dedicated beat-analysis runtime after the editor scripts
- Reads the selected local audio file or the current browser audio source
- Supports stereo and multichannel audio by creating a mono analysis signal
- Generates a detailed waveform
- Uses adaptive transient and energy detection
- Produces normal and strong/drop beat markers
- Restores markers to the Beat Markers timeline lane
- Updates marker counts, waveform summary and downloadable analysis controls
- Dispatches the beat-complete event used by the professional timeline

Auto-Sync restored:
- Auto-Sync reads the restored beat-marker DOM directly
- Selecting Auto-Sync before analysis automatically performs analysis first
- Camera movement and timed visual regions are regenerated after analysis
- Timed effects appear in the Effects lane
- True video-frame zoom, rotation, impact and camera shake remain supported

Shift+K razor:
- Hover over the video timeline and press Shift+K to split the selected project time
- Hover over the music timeline and press Shift+K to split the music
- A visible razor guide follows the pointer
- Video splits preserve source start/end values for preview and direct rendering
- Split video sections share the original source file without duplicating media
- Music splits appear above the waveform

Left/right deletion:
- Hover a split boundary
- Select ×L to delete the section on the left
- Select R× to delete the section on the right
- Video sections ripple together after deletion
- Music sections are removed from preview and the Web Audio export mix
- Double-click a removed music section to restore it

Preserved:
- Ripple cuts and clip snapping
- Undo and redo
- Professional timeline scrolling and middle-mouse panning
- Multiple video lanes and transitions
- Fire, explosion and smoke VFX
- Multilayer text, logo and image editor
