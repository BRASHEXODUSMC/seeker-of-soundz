Seeker Of SoundZ v4.31.1
PRODUCER HUB 5.6.1 — RANGE PLACEMENT & TRIM FIX

Range placement:
- Timed Effects, Auto-Sync, Cut & Edit, and Professional tools now sit outside
  the scrolling timeline viewport
- Project Start/End range sits beneath those controls and directly above tracks
- Playhead begins at the track area and does not cross editor controls
- Range handles use the exact visible range rectangle
- Removed duplicate legacy range-drag listeners

Clip trimming:
- Left and right trim handles receive reliable pointer input
- Trim movement uses the exact visible timeline width
- Normal and Ripple modes trim freely
- Magnetic mode snaps to other clip edges and beat markers
- The clip's own current edge is excluded from snap candidates
- Source start/end values update correctly
- Trimmed clip reloads from its new source start
- Status reports the new clip duration
- Undo remains supported

No Supabase update is required.
