The Story

Seeking new sounds and unforgettable experiences.

Seeker Of SoundZ is a DJ, electronic music producer, artist, and creator focused on exploring the emotion and atmosphere hidden inside sound.

The project was built around curiosity. Every track, live performance, video, and visual is part of an ongoing search for new energy, new ideas, and new ways to connect with listeners.

From powerful live sets to detailed studio sessions, the goal is to create experiences that feel immersive, personal, and memorable.

I do not only want people to hear the music. I want them to feel like they entered another atmosphere.
